## Description

For more than 98% of human history there was no money at all. No coins, no wages, no prices. So what did people actually do all day?

In this video we follow a day without money, using archaeology and studies of people who lived mostly by foraging (like the Hadza and the Ju/'hoansi). We look at how many hours they really worked (it's debated), what they talked about by the fire, how they traded across hundreds of kilometers without coins, and what they saved instead of money.

Where experts still disagree, the video says so.
